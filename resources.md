## Books recommended by the UP Library

- Becker, L. and Denicolo, P. *Developing Research Proposals*. SAGE, 2012.
- Ogden, T. E. and Goldberg, I. A. (eds). *Research Proposals: A Guide to Success*. Academic Press, 2002.

## Further reading on research craft

- Booth, W. C., Colomb, G. G., Williams, J. M., Bizup, J. and FitzGerald, W. T. *The Craft of Research*. 4th ed. University of Chicago Press, 2016. The best general treatment of turning a topic into a question and a question into an argument.
- Heilmeier, G. H. The Heilmeier Catechism. DARPA. The eight questions in docs/01.
- Alley, M. *The Craft of Scientific Writing*. 4th ed. Springer, 2018.

## Verification, validation and uncertainty (computational work)

- Oberkampf, W. L. and Roy, C. J. *Verification and Validation in Scientific Computing*. Cambridge University Press, 2010.
- Roache, P. J. *Verification and Validation in Computational Science and Engineering*. Hermosa, 1998.
- ASME V&V 20-2009. *Standard for Verification and Validation in Computational Fluid Dynamics and Heat Transfer*. ASME.
- Celik, I. B., Ghia, U., Roache, P. J., Freitas, C. J., Coleman, H. and Raad, P. E. Procedure for estimation and reporting of uncertainty due to discretization in CFD applications. *Journal of Fluids Engineering* 130(7), 2008.

## Tools

- Overleaf (LaTeX), with the Lab template in `templates/proposal-template.tex`.
- Zotero for references; keep one `.bib` for the whole degree.
- Mermaid for Gantt charts (renders on GitHub); see `templates/gantt-template.md`.
- Pandoc for Markdown to LaTeX conversion.

All references above should be verified against the original source before being cited in a proposal.
