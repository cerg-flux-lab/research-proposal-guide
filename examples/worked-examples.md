# Worked examples: weak versus strong

Illustrative only. The strong versions are written to show the *shape* of a good statement; the numbers and references are placeholders and must not be copied into a real proposal.

## Working title

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| A study of machine learning in CFD | Physics-informed neural networks for laminar flow around bluff bodies: accuracy and cost relative to finite-volume solutions across Reynolds number 10 to 200 |
| Investigation into hydrocyclone performance | Prediction of air-core formation in hydrocyclones using a multiphase Lattice Boltzmann model: validation across the industrial operating range |
| Quantum computing for fluid dynamics | A Carleman-linearised quantum algorithm for the one-dimensional Burgers equation: resource estimates and classical emulation up to 20 qubits |

## Problem statement

**Weak.** "Cooling of silicon detectors is a challenging problem that has received much attention. This study will use CFD to investigate it."

**Strong.** "The ATLAS Inner Tracker relies on dry nitrogen flushing to keep the dew point in the detector volume below the evaporative cooling temperature of minus 35 degrees Celsius. Published models of the flushing transient [refs] assume a well-mixed volume and predict dry-out times that disagree with commissioning measurements by a factor of two. No study has resolved the spatial distribution of moisture during dry-out, so the location and duration of condensation risk during cooling start-up cannot currently be predicted."

Why it works: setting, quantity (dew point), a number (factor of two), a named failure (well-mixed assumption), a missing study, and a consequence (unpredictable condensation risk).

## Research question

**Weak.** "Does the LBM work for multiphase flow?"

**Strong.** "To what accuracy, relative to the LDA measurements of [ref], can a free-energy Lattice Boltzmann model predict air-core diameter and tangential velocity in a 75 mm hydrocyclone across inlet velocities of 5 to 15 m/s, without case-specific tuning of the interface parameters?"

## Objectives

**Weak.**
1. Develop a solver.
2. Validate the solver.
3. Study the effect of parameters.

**Strong.**
1. Implement a D3Q19 free-energy Lattice Boltzmann solver in PyTorch and verify it against the Laplace law, the Rayleigh-Taylor instability growth rate and a mesh-converged finite-volume solution, each to within 1%.
2. Validate the solver's prediction of tangential velocity at three axial stations against the LDA dataset of [ref], reporting error relative to the published experimental uncertainty.
3. Quantify the sensitivity of predicted air-core diameter to inlet velocity (5 to 15 m/s) and spigot diameter (10 to 25 mm) across a 20-case test matrix, with discretisation uncertainty estimated by the grid convergence index.
4. Compare the wall-clock cost per converged case against the finite-volume baseline on a single GPU and report the accuracy-cost trade-off.

## Method justification

**Weak.** "The Lattice Boltzmann Method will be used because it is a modern and powerful technique."

**Strong.** "The Lattice Boltzmann Method is chosen over volume-of-fluid finite-volume interface capturing for two reasons specific to this problem. First, the air-liquid interface emerges from the free-energy functional rather than being reconstructed, which removes the interface compression parameter that [ref] identified as the dominant source of tuning sensitivity. Second, the collision step is local, so the solver maps efficiently to a single GPU, which the test matrix in Section 5.2 requires. The trade-off is a limited stable density ratio; the mitigation in Section 8.4 applies."

## Risk entry

**Weak.** "Risk: solver instability. Mitigation: monitor closely."

**Strong.** "Risk: the free-energy model becomes unstable at the air-water density ratio of about 800. Likelihood: medium. Impact: high (Objectives 2 to 4 depend on it). Mitigation: begin at density ratio 100 to establish the pipeline; adopt the entropic or cascaded collision operator reported stable to ratio 1000 by [ref]; if still unstable by month 8, reduce scope to the ratio range the model supports and document the limitation."
