# 09. Common mistakes

Ranked roughly by how often they send a proposal back for revision.

1. **No identifiable gap.** The literature review summarises but never says what is missing. Fix: end every review subsection with "However, ..." and collect those sentences into the problem statement.

2. **Objectives that are activities.** "Develop a model", "study the effect", "investigate". Fix: rewrite each as a deliverable with a metric and a tolerance.

3. **Method asserted, not justified.** "ANSYS Fluent will be used." Why? What alternative was rejected? Fix: one sentence of justification per major choice.

4. **No validation plan, or "validation against experiments" with no dataset named.** Fix: name the dataset, the quantities compared and the tolerance, now.

5. **Scope that is a career, not a degree.** Fix: delimitations section; the Heilmeier questions; a test matrix with a cost estimate.

6. **A timeline with no slack and no writing.** Fix: 15 to 20% contingency, writing bars from month three, milestones as deliverables.

7. **Citing papers you have not read.** It shows in the viva. Fix: open every PDF; if an AI tool suggested it, verify it exists and says what you think it says.

8. **Title and problem statement that do not match.** The title promises a general method; the problem statement describes a single case. Fix: write the title last, from the problem statement.

9. **Background that starts with the invention of the computer.** Fix: start at the level of your sub-field; two paragraphs to reach the specific problem.

10. **Undefined symbols and inconsistent units.** Fix: nomenclature list; one pass checking every equation for dimensional consistency.

11. **Preliminary results omitted out of modesty.** Even a verified Poiseuille profile from your own code is feasibility evidence. Fix: include it, labelled preliminary.

12. **Risks listed without mitigations you control.** "Monitor closely" is not a mitigation. Fix: for each risk, a concrete fallback.

13. **AI-generated prose.** It reads smoothly and says nothing specific; committees notice, and the viva exposes it. Fix: follow the Guardrails in chapter 07. Your proposal is Tier 1.

14. **Sending the first draft to your supervisor without self-review.** Fix: run `checklists/self-review-checklist.md` first. Every item you catch yourself is one your supervisor does not have to.
